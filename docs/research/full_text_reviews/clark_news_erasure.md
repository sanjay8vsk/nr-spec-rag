## 2. Objective and contribution

The problem is keeping an LM system's answers current as the world changes. Conventional RAG "has focused on improving model behavior during prediction through better retrieval or reasoning" and simply appends new documents to the store, leaving version conflicts to be resolved (or not) implicitly at query time. ERASE instead acts at **document-insertion time**: "incrementally deleting or rewriting other entries in the knowledge base each time a document is added", i.e., "shifting the cost of reasoning about consistency from query-time to insertion-time" (Limitations/§7).

- **Method contribution:** ERASE, a KB-editing procedure (details §5 below). Prediction afterwards is standard RAG over the edited KB.
- **Benchmark contribution:** CLARK. CLARK-News measures whether a system answering questions **about the current state of a chronologically streamed news corpus** stays accurate as facts change; the camera-ready adds one probe of whether systems can still recover **initial-timestamp answers** after the full stream (Table 4).

## 3. CLARK-News construction (App B.1, Tables 6–8, 12)

- **World states:** Wikidata `(subj, rel)` pairs with ≥2 distinct objects at different timestamps, subjects filtered to English-speaking countries, retrieved via SPARQL; changes fall "between November 2021 and April 2024". Totals (Table 12): **533 (subj, rel) pairs, 1,174 changed triples, 10 relation types**, heavily skewed: member of sports team 284 pairs/382 triples, position held 164/382, employer 38/77, chairperson 20/42, head of state 9/18, CEO 6/13, unmarried partner 5/12, residence 4/8, headquarters 2/4, member of political party 1/2.
- **Evidence documents:** one source article per timestamped triple, crowd-collected on Prolific in **two human rounds plus one LM round**: round 1 selects a Google-News-scraped (or hand-found) article that implies the fact "such that a reasonable person, without any prior knowledge, can infer that the fact is true", written inside the fact's validity window, main-body text only, English, web page, and — critically — **avoiding articles that state the fact is or is about to become false** and avoiding any stated succeeding fact; round 2 independently re-affirms (failures discarded); round 3 is an LM check for the same conditions. Result: **1,149 articles** (footnote 5: "1149 < 1174, meaning at least a few articles were shared across relations – these represent difficult cases where a single article makes multiple relation changes").
- **Annotator protocol/count/agreement:** 680 US English-speaking Prolific annotators, average 16:50 minutes on task, average pay $14.20/hour, IRB-exempt, no PII. Round 2 acts as a pass/fail filter; **no inter-annotator agreement statistic is reported for CLARK-News**. (The 95% validation accuracy, 3 author-annotators over 3 conversations / 1,008 questions, belongs to CLARK-Conversations only.)
- **Questions:** template-generated per relation (Table 8; wh- and yes/no forms), **1,409 questions** total. **Templates contain no time expressions** ("Who is the CEO of {subject}?"); the evaluation timestamp is injected by the inference prompt, not the question text.
- **Gold and historical answers:** every `(subj, rel, obj)` carries start/end validity timestamps, so the full answer progression per question is recorded: "As all answers are labeled with a start and end timestamp, we always know which answer is true for a given timestamp" (§5.1). Footnote 6 limits the semantics: validity "does not correspond to when these facts became true and false in the real world, but rather to when the article introducing the changed fact was written and read" — validity time is **ingestion time**, not real-world change time.
- **Old vs new evidence; traceability:** each answer version keeps its own inducing article with its validity window — this is Table 1's "Attributed answers": CLARK "ensures that the version-attributed source texts containing the answer have been extracted". So old and new evidence texts are preserved separately **in the dataset**, and every answer version is traceable to a source article. This is dataset-level provenance; nothing at evaluation time asks the model to produce or match it (§8).

## 4. Temporal QA design

- **Target time per question:** supplied only by the inference prompt wrapper — "answer the following question at timestep {ts}" (App A.3) — never by the question text.
- **Main schedule (rolling present):** articles are streamed in timestamp order; questions are asked "at regular intervals corresponding to when 20%, 40%, 60%, 80%, and 100% of the total world state changes have been revealed". At each checkpoint the model answers **all** questions whose answers have changed (Q) plus an equal-sized sample of unchanged questions (Q′). The target at every checkpoint is the **state as of that checkpoint**: the same question is re-asked at multiple time points, and its gold answer changes as the corpus grows — but the requested target is always "current as of now", never a past state.
- **Historical targets (Table 4 only, camera-ready only):** "we ask RAG and ERASE Mixtral LMs to recover the answer to a question at the initial timestamp, given the state of the knowledge base, first at the initial timestamp, then at the final timestamp." The Final-TS column is a genuine **old-target question over a knowledge store that also contains all newer information**: Mixtral+RAG falls 85.1% → 58.2%, Mixtral+ERASE 85.9% → 64.8%; "ERASE systems decay less than RAG at reasoning about old information in the presence of new information." Scope limits: one experiment, one model (Mixtral-8x7B), one domain (news), two systems, aggregate accuracy only, evidence state not experimentally controlled.
- **Symmetry:** strongly asymmetric. The entire main evaluation is present-anchored; the single old-target probe is a secondary analysis.
- **Three timelines:** ingestion time (article timestamps, drives streaming), question time (checkpoint), answer-validity time (identical to ingestion time by footnote 6). Real-world change time is explicitly out of scope.
- **Historical facts in the store:** the extraction prompt keeps only current-state text ("Only include facts referring to the current world state (what is true *now*), as opposed to facts true in the past", App A.2); history survives as per-fact (timestamp, truth-value) annotations shown at inference. The RAG baseline corpus, by contrast, keeps all article text verbatim.

## 5. ERASE methodology and baselines

- **KB representation:** entries are (fact f_j, history H_j = [(τ, truth-value), …]).
- **Per-document update (App A.1):** (1) *Retrieve* facts related to the new document (GTR dense retrieval, similarity > 0.7); (2) *Update*, two passes: first classify each retrieved fact as reinforce / no change / make false — with the conservative default "Assume that the fact is still true (keep true) if nothing in the input contradicts it" — then rewrite each now-false fact into a true one conditioned on the document plus the still-true facts, which enables **multi-hop edits** (the paper's example: Mary moves to Amazon ⟹ rewrite "Mary is coworkers with Bob" to "Mary is coworkers with Quinn") and partial-falsity salvage; (3) *Add* newly extracted atomic, decontextualized current-state facts.
- **Contradiction handling:** resolved at insertion time by the classification pass. App D.1 documents the failure mode of the conservative default: a retrieved fact needing update was kept because "it is not directly contradicted by the input" (under-updating).
- **Removes outdated knowledge, preserves history, or both?** Both, in a specific split: outdated **text** is rewritten away from the retrieval-facing fact store, while the outdated **value's history** is preserved as (τ, truth-value) annotations that the inference prompt displays ("{f_i} ({v_{i0}} at {tau_{i0}}, {v_{i1}} at {tau_{i1}}, ...)"). Old evidence *documents* are not preserved in ERASE's store (they are in the RAG baseline's).
- **KB maintenance vs answer generation:** the paper evaluates KB maintenance *through* answer generation — prediction is standard RAG over the edited KB, and QA accuracy is the only lens; there is no intrinsic KB-quality metric. Table 5 partially decomposes stages post hoc (error analysis).
- **Baselines:** RAG (passage retrieval; Lewis et al. 2020), Fact-RAG (retrieval over extracted facts, no editing; Chen et al. 2023), full-context (Mixtral long-context; skyline for conversations, fails to scale on news), and Self-RAG stacking (Table 2: Llama-2 + Self-RAG 36.8% accuracy / 44:15 runtime → +ERASE 47.9% / 6:41; the prose "an 11% improvement" is 11.1 percentage points).

## 6. Evaluation protocol

- **Systems:** Mixtral-8x7B-Instruct (queried via Together AI) and Llama-3-8B-Instruct (one A100), temperature 0, zero-shot chain-of-thought, GTR (T5-large) retriever with fast inner-product search (Douze et al. 2024, i.e., the FAISS library — reviewer identification), context deliberately capped at 4096 (news) / 2048 (conversations) tokens "to simulate a (realistic) scenario where the total number of changes cannot fit into the context window" (footnote 8). Footnote 7: Llama-3's knowledge cutoff is March 2023 and Mixtral's "likely late 2022 or early 2023", so part of the Nov 2021–Apr 2024 window exceeds both models' cutoffs; the paper does not analyze parametric contamination further.
- **Answer format and scoring:** every news question is **multiple choice "with challenging alternatives: the model must select between all answers that have been true for the question in the past, present, or future"**, scored by exact match. List questions (conversations) are scored by set equality over a JSON list that "may be empty, [], if none of the answers are true". **No abstention option exists in the news protocol**; the empty-list affordance is the only "none" output anywhere, and it is not scored as abstention.
- **Denominators/schedule:** per checkpoint, all changed questions + equal-sized unchanged sample (§4 above).
- **Reported results:** news results are **figure-only** (Figure 4 accuracy curves per checkpoint; no numeric table, no error bars, no significance tests); headline: ERASE beats baselines "by 7–13% (Mixtral-8x7B) and 6–10% (Llama-3-8B) absolute". Conversations: Table 3 reports mean ± standard error with bold marking "the statistically significantly best" per (model, subset, #updates) — e.g., Llama-3 single-hop 1-update: ERASE 65.3±1.7 vs RAG 57.8±1.8; multi-hop stays hard (26.5±0.2 vs 22.2±0.1); the full-context skyline wins conversations outright (e.g., Mixtral multi-hop 1-update 43.2±1.7 vs ERASE 20.1±1.4). Notably, at 0 updates ERASE is slightly *below* RAG (Mixtral 82.0 vs 86.0; Llama-3 82.0 vs 84.4): editing costs accuracy on unchanged facts and pays off only once answers change. Single runs at temperature 0; no repeat runs.
- **Stage-wise error analysis (Table 5):** 30 error cases (ERASE, Llama-3, last news timestep), overlapping categories: Retrieval during Updating 8 (40%), LM Reasoning during Inference 7 (35%), Extraction 6 (30%), Retrieval during Inference 6 (30%), Other 6 (30%). **Internal inconsistency:** the caption says "thirty error cases" but every count/percentage pair implies a denominator of twenty (8→40%, 7→35%, 6→30%); counts sum to 33 because categories overlap. One of caption or percentages is wrong; the paper does not resolve it.
- **Human vs automated:** evaluation is fully automated (exact match / set equality); human effort is confined to dataset construction.
- **Paper's own Limitations:** multi-hop retrieval and editing remain weak; LM-driven edits "may introduce noise into the knowledge base" that "has the potential to snowball" at large scale; per-document update cost is justified only when insertions ≪ queries.

## 7. Explicit six-cell comparison

Evidence regime first (per protocol rule, do not equate chronological accumulation with controlled evidence manipulation): CLARK-News evidence is **never experimentally assigned**. For the RAG arm it is the naturally accumulated chronological article corpus up to the checkpoint; for the ERASE arm it is an **incrementally LM-edited fact store** (current-state text + history annotations). The gold answer is *defined by* the ingestion stream (footnote 6), so in the main evaluation target and evidence co-move and cannot be decoupled: a current-target question whose corpus lacks the current article is impossible by construction. The sole decoupling is Table 4, which fixes the target at the initial timestamp while the store advances to the final state. Our cells use T = target version ∈ {old, new} × S = evidence context ∈ {old-only, new-only, both}.

| Cell | NR-SpecRAG condition | CLARK-News status |
|---|---|---|
| A | old target, old-only evidence | **Not evaluated.** Table 4's Init-TS column is current-target relative to its store (question time = store time), i.e., a D-analogue control at an early world state, not an old-target/old-evidence condition. |
| B | new target, old-only evidence | **Not evaluated** — impossible by construction in the main protocol: the gold flips to "new" only at the moment the new article is ingested, so a new-target question never faces an old-only store. |
| C | old target, new-only evidence | **Not evaluated.** (Closest object: ERASE's final store is current-state *text* only, but the old values persist as history annotations shown at inference, so it is not a new-only condition either.) |
| D | new target, new-only evidence | **Partially represented, trivially:** unchanged-question subset Q′ and pre-change checkpoints, where only one version exists; a sampling regime, not a controlled arm. |
| E | old target, both-versions evidence | **Partially represented (uncontrolled):** Table 4 Final-TS column — explicit initial-timestamp target over the full final store containing old+new information (RAG: all articles verbatim; ERASE: new text + old values in annotations). Natural mixture, one model/domain, two systems, aggregate accuracy only, camera-ready only. |
| F | new target, both-versions evidence | **Partially represented (uncontrolled):** changed-question subset at post-change checkpoints — current-target questions over a corpus that still contains the superseded articles. Mixture composition is whatever the stream produced; never varied as a factor. |

**Directly evaluated cells: 0/6.** Evidence classification: *naturally retrieved from an incrementally accumulated corpus* (RAG arm) / *incrementally edited* (ERASE arm); never fixed per item, never assigned to old-only/new-only/mixed conditions, never crossed with target version. No condition-wise contrast isolates evidence composition as the cause of any accuracy difference.

## 8. Version and attribution assessment

- **Explicit historical-target questions?** Yes — but only Table 4 (timestamp injected via prompt), and only as a secondary probe. The main evaluation is entirely present-anchored.
- **Source version per answer?** In the **dataset**, yes: every answer version is linked to its inducing article and validity window ("Attributed answers", Table 1 — per that same table, TimeQA also has answer attribution). In the **evaluation**, no: the model selects a multiple-choice option; nothing checks which source or version the model relied on, and no attribution metric exists.
- **Correct-historical vs current answer distinguished?** In the gold, yes (validity windows). In scoring, only through choice identity — and here CLARK-News has a structurally interesting property: since **every distractor is an answer that was true at some time**, essentially every multiple-choice error is a wrong-time/wrong-version selection. Version confusion is the measured error mode *by construction*. But errors are never broken down by direction (picked an older vs newer answer), and accuracy is the only statistic.
- **Outdated-supported answers identified?** No. Table 5 codes *system stage* (retrieval/updating/extraction/reasoning), not evidence-version behaviour; there is no unsupported / contradicted / outdated-supported / mixed-version coding.
- **Citations resolved? Citation correctness?** No citations exist anywhere in the protocol — answers are option selections, not cited generations. Nothing resembling E1–E5 or clause-level verification is possible in this format.
- **Normative/deontic language?** None. All facts are descriptive news facts (employer, CEO, sports team, residence…); no obligation/permission language, no normative documents.
- **Verdict on the brief's caution:** confirmed — CLARK-News's temporal QA does **not** constitute version-attribution evaluation. Attribution exists as dataset metadata only; the metric never inspects the version basis of the model's answer.

## 9. C1–C4 reassessment

Using the series definitions: C1 = version-conditional grounding; C2 = normative-modality preservation; C3 = citation-to-clause reliability; C4 = sufficiency and calibrated abstention.

- **C1 — NARROWED, survives.** New overlaps this paper creates: (i) the camera-ready Table 4 is the **first historical-target evaluation in the reviewed literature** — a claim that "no prior work asks old-target questions over a store containing newer information" is now false; (ii) re-asking the same question at multiple knowledge states (checkpoint design) is established; (iii) using other-version true answers as distractors — version confusion as the error signal by construction — is established; (iv) dataset-level per-answer version-attributed sources are established (CLARK, and TimeQA per CLARK's Table 1). What no reviewed paper does, CLARK-News included: **controlled evidence-version conditions** (old-only/new-only/mixed as assigned factors — CLARK's evidence is the natural stream or an edited store; HoH controls evidence but only for current targets), **old-target × controlled-evidence cells (A/C/E as designed conditions)** — Table 4's E-analogue is a one-off uncontrolled probe, **per-answer or per-citation version attribution as a scored output**, **behaviour coding under version conflict**, **free-form cited generation**, and **authoritative normative versioned specifications** as the corpus.
  - *Does CLARK-News weaken the old-target half of the six-cell design (the brief's focal question)?* It weakens the *novelty of asking* old-target questions: Table 4 demonstrates the phenomenon (old-answer recovery degrades as the store accumulates newer information, RAG 58.2% vs ERASE 64.8%). It does **not** weaken the design itself: Table 4 cannot localize *why* failures occur (evidence uncontrolled, no attribution, MC format), which is precisely what controlled cells A/C/E plus VFR/CVS/VCDR/VAA deliver. The six-cell design remains justified and is in fact motivated by the paper's own observation that "the knowledge base becomes more noisy as it becomes contaminated with outdated knowledge. Conflicting, outdated knowledge may be retrieved".
- **C2 — untouched.** No normative or deontic content anywhere.
- **C3 — untouched.** No citation mechanism at all.
- **C4 — essentially untouched.** The news protocol forces a choice among temporally-true alternatives with no abstain option; the conversations empty-list affordance is a narrow "none apply" output, not calibrated abstention; no confidence, coverage, or risk reporting.

## 10. Cross-paper synthesis (seven full-text reviews)

- **vs HoH (hoh.md):** complementary on the two axes that define C1. HoH: controlled evidence mixtures ({R}, {R,O}, {O} + ordering), current targets only, real revision conflicts, abstention scored, no attribution. CLARK-News: natural chronological accumulation, rolling current targets plus one uncontrolled old-target probe, dataset-level attribution, no abstention. HoH's Table 1 claim that CLARK is the only other benchmark with both evidence-text and outdated-information annotations is consistent with what we verified here (Table 1 "Attributed answers ✓" + per-version articles). Note HoH cites this work as "Li et al. 2024" (arXiv v1), a version that did not yet contain the forgetting experiment.
- **vs VersionRAG (versionrag.md):** VersionRAG asks version-explicit questions ("in version X…") over versioned software docs under natural retrieval, no evidence control, single-axis accuracy; CLARK-News never names versions in questions (timestamp injected in the prompt) and adds the temporal-stream dimension VersionRAG lacks. Neither controls evidence composition; neither attributes answers.
- **vs DocNavigator (research_square_rs8854256.md):** DocNavigator *defines* a cross-version error rate and never measures it; CLARK-News actually measures temporal confusion — but only as aggregate MC accuracy, not as a version-attribution metric.
- **vs TelcoAI / Chat3GPP (telcoai.md, chat3gpp.md):** those cover the 3GPP domain with no version axis; CLARK-News covers the temporal axis with no telecom/normative dimension. The intersection (versioned normative 3GPP evaluation) remains empty.
- **vs RAGChecker (ragchecker.md):** claim-level entailment metrics but version-blind; CLARK-News is version-aware in its gold but claim-blind in its scoring (exact-match MC). No reviewed paper combines claim-level analysis with version conditioning.
- **Citation-chase assessment (no new leads opened):** CLARK's Table 1 benchmarks — SituatedQA, TimeQA, TempLAMA, RealTimeQA, FreshQA, PAT-Questions — were assessed against C1's falsification condition (a version-attribution metric over authoritative versioned documents under controlled evidence conditions). Per that table and the paper's §2, none combines changing answers + attributed sources + cross-source updating (TimeQA: attributed+changing but within-article, no cross-source updating, single-snapshot Wikipedia reading comprehension; RealTimeQA/FreshQA/PAT-Questions: present-anchored; TempLAMA: parametric cloze probing). None can satisfy the falsification condition, so they are background citations for the thesis, not gating reviews; no CH lead was opened.

## 11. Reviewer verdict

- **Confirmed facts (from the camera-ready full text):** peer-reviewed at Findings NAACL 2025, pp. 3070–3090; CLARK-News = 533 (s,r) pairs / 1,174 changes / 1,149 tri-validated articles / 1,409 template questions / 10 relations / Nov 2021–Apr 2024; answer validity = ingestion time (footnote 6); evaluation = chronological streaming with questions at 20/40/60/80/100% checkpoints, MC among all temporally-true answers, exact match; ERASE = insertion-time KB editing with truth-history annotations; news gains 7–13 / 6–10 points absolute (figure-only, no uncertainty); conversations Table 3 with ±SE and significance bolding; Table 4 old-target probe RAG 85.1→58.2 vs ERASE 85.9→64.8 (Mixtral, news); Table 2 Self-RAG stack 36.8→47.9 with ~7× runtime reduction; no abstention, no citations, no normative content, no controlled evidence conditions, no answer-level attribution.
- **Provisional interpretations (reviewer's, labeled as such):** (a) Table 4's questions presumably reuse the App A.3 inference prompt with ts = initial timestamp — the paper does not print a separate prompt for this experiment; (b) "fast inner-product search datastructure (Douze et al., 2024)" is the FAISS library; (c) Table 5's percentages imply a denominator of 20 against a caption stating thirty cases — we treat the category *ranking*, not the percentages, as reliable; (d) the 0-update accuracy drop under ERASE is editing-induced noise on unchanged facts, consistent with the paper's own Limitations.
- **Unresolved questions (not answerable from the paper):** exact news accuracy values (Figure 4 is the only source and its text layer carries no readable data points); which template forms (wh vs yes/no) were instantiated for news and the news MC choice-set sizes; the Table 5 denominator; whether MC options were shuffled.
- **C1 status: NARROWED — survives.** CLARK-News adds real prior art on the old-target *question* (Table 4) and on version-confusion-by-construction scoring, but provides no controlled evidence conditions, no answer-level version attribution, no behaviour coding, no citations, and no normative corpus. Combined with HoH (controlled evidence, current targets only) and VersionRAG (version targets, uncontrolled evidence), the two C1 axes have each been touched separately; **no prior work crosses them, attributes answers to versions, or instantiates any of it on normative specification text. The six-cell design remains justified** — it is the controlled crossing that none of the seven reviewed papers performs.
- **Wordings now forbidden in the thesis** (additions to the hoh.md §12 catalogue): "first to evaluate historical-version targets" / "no prior work asks what *was* true once newer information exists" (CLARK Table 4); "first benchmark to re-ask the same question at multiple knowledge states" (CLARK checkpoints); "first to use other-version answers as distractors" (CLARK MC design); "first dataset linking each answer version to its source text" (CLARK "attributed answers"; also TimeQA per CLARK Table 1).
- **Recommended C1 gap wording (exact):** *"No prior work evaluates version-conditional grounding as a controlled experiment that (i) fixes the version composition of the evidence context (old-only / new-only / mixed) independently of (ii) the requested target version (current and historical), over (iii) authoritative normative specification text, while (iv) scoring per-answer and per-citation version attribution and (v) coding answer behaviour under version conflict. The nearest priors each supply one piece: HoH (Ouyang et al., 2025) controls evidence mixtures for current targets only; CLARK-News (Li et al., 2025) poses rolling current-target questions plus a single uncontrolled historical-target retention probe, with dataset-level rather than answer-level source attribution; VersionRAG (2025) scores version-explicit questions under natural retrieval without evidence control or attribution."*
- **CH-12 disposition: INCLUDED** (full-text reviewed, FT-07; must be cited and distinguished in the thesis as the historical-target and attribution-annotation prior).
- **Documented paper-internal issues (for citation accuracy, not criticism):** Table 5 thirty-vs-twenty denominator inconsistency; camera-ready footnote 1 still anonymized; "an 11% improvement" (Table 2 discussion) is 11.1 percentage points.
